# AI

# ⚙️ AI Technologies
## 1. Machine Learning (ML) aka Learning methods of AI `(इंसान के जैसा सीखना)`


#### 1. Supervised Learning 
Learns from correct examples. (छोटे बच्चे को पहले से सही जवाब सिखाना)


#### 2. Unsupervised Learning 
Finds patterns by itself. (छोटे बच्चे को खुद patterns पहचानने देना)


#### 3. Reinforcement Learning 
Learns from rewards and mistakes. “Remove wrong actions and keep correct actions.” (छोटे बच्चे को गलतियों और इनाम से सिखाना)


#### 4. Deep Learning
Uses brain-like ***Neural networks*** to learn complex things. (छोटे बच्चे को बार-बार अभ्यास और अनुभव से सिखाना)


## 2. Natural Language Processing (NLP)
It is like how to speak with language. (इंसान के जैसा बोलना और समझना)


## 3. Computer Vision
Helps AI see and understand images and videos. (इंसान के जैसा देखना)


## 4. Robotics
Uses AI to control machines and robots to do physical work. (इंसान के हाथों जैसा करना)


## 5. Expert Systems 
AI that follows expert-made rules to do specific tasks. eg. - Medical diagnosis, Technical support. (इंसान के जैसा विशेषज्ञ निर्णय लेना — Doctor, Engineer)


## 6. Fuzzy Logic
Helps AI work with uncertain or partial information. (इंसान के जैसा अंदाज़ा लगाना या अधूरी जानकारी में निर्णय लेना)

# Type of AI
## 🧠 Types of AI (by capability)
- Narrow AI (Weak AI)
- Designed for a specific task
- Example: voice assistants, recommendation systems
- General AI (AGI) (not fully achieved yet)
- Can perform any intellectual task like humans
- Superintelligent AI (theoretical)
- Surpasses human intelligence

## 🧩 Types of AI (by function)
- Reactive Machines – No memory (e.g., chess AI)
- Limited Memory – Learns from past data (e.g., self-driving cars)
- Theory of Mind – Understands emotions (still experimental)
- Self-aware AI – Fully conscious (not real yet)

## 💼 AI Used in Different Fields
- Healthcare AI – disease diagnosis, medical imaging
- Finance AI – fraud detection, trading bots
- Education AI – tutoring systems, grading
- Entertainment AI – game AI, content creation
- Business AI – chatbots, automation
