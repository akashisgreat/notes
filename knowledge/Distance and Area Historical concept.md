## 1. Feet

## 2. Miles
1000 steps walking distance. 

**1,000 Paces:** In ancient Rome, a "pace" (_passus_) was measured as a **double step**—the distance from where one foot hits the ground to when that _same_ foot hits the ground again. This equals about 5 ft (1.5 meters)

```
Distance = (Natural Distance covered by 1000 steps)
1 Mile = 5280 ft
```
##### `1 Mile = 1.609143 km`

## 1. Acre

Oxen Plowing land area in one day.

**The Length (660 ft):** The long side of the strip matched how far oxen could walk and plow before needing a rest. This distance became known as a **furlong** (meaning "a furrow long"), which is 220 yards or 660 ft.

```
1 Furlong   = Distance of Oxen pull a plow, in one go (without rest). 
		 = 660 ft ( 1/8th of 1 Mile ) 
		 = (10 Gunter's chain length) ***
		 = 220 yads
```

**The Width (66 ft):** The short side was standardized as 4 rods or perches, equal to 22 yards or 66 feet.

##### `1 Acre = 660 ft x 66 ft = 43560 sq ft`

### 1.1 Dismil

`1 Arce = 100 Dismil`

1 Dismil = 43560 / 100 = 435.6 sq ft
